Privacy, Data Protection & Legal Compliance Policy

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

KaagazSeva is committed to structured, lawful, and transparent handling of personal information. This document defines the standards under which user data is collected, processed, stored, protected, and governed.

1. Platform Positioning & Legal Nature

KaagazSeva operates as an independent government service assistance platform. We are not affiliated with any government authority. We assist users in preparing, managing, and facilitating applications.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity information (Name, Mobile, Email)
  • Residential and regional details (State, District, Address)
  • Uploaded documents for service processing
  • Application and transaction records
  • Agent activity logs and processing history
  • Technical access logs (IP, timestamps, device metadata)

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Service execution and application processing
  • Authorized agent assignment
  • Fraud prevention and risk monitoring
  • Operational analytics and compliance auditing
  • Legal obligations under applicable Indian law

4. Data Encryption & Security Architecture

We implement a structured security framework including:

  • Role-based access control
  • Encrypted storage protocols
  • Watermarked document access tracking
  • Time-bound access permissions
  • Comprehensive audit logs
  • Automated monitoring of agent activity
Any unauthorized access, duplication, distribution, or misuse of documents results in immediate suspension and potential legal proceedings under applicable Indian laws.

5. GDPR-Style User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • Access personal data stored
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion where legally permissible
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential processing
  • Raise grievance regarding misuse
Requests may be submitted via official support contact.

6. Data Retention Policy

Data is retained strictly for operational, legal, and audit purposes. Upon completion of service cycles, documents may be archived securely or anonymized where appropriate.

7. Payment & Financial Data

Payments are processed via secure third-party gateways. KaagazSeva does not store card numbers, CVV, or banking credentials.

8. Breach Notification Clause

In the unlikely event of a verified data breach affecting personal information, affected users will be notified in a timely manner. Appropriate containment and corrective measures will be executed immediately.

9. Compliance with Indian Law

This platform operates under the framework of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and other applicable Indian data protection regulations. Users agree that disputes shall fall under Indian jurisdiction.

10. Jurisdiction & Governing Law

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by the laws of India. Any dispute arising shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts within India.

11. Founder & Platform Authority

KaagazSeva operates under structured administrative governance led by its Founder. All data governance decisions are monitored under centralized audit controls.

12. Updates to Policy

We reserve the right to modify this policy to align with evolving regulatory and operational requirements. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of updates.
