Refund Policy

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

1. Service Charge Structure

Payments include:

  • Government Fee (non-refundable once submitted)
  • Platform Service Charge
  • Optional Doorstep Charges

2. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Government rejection due to user error
  • Incorrect documents provided by user
  • Change of mind after processing started
  • Government fee already submitted

3. Eligible Refund Scenarios

  • Platform technical failure
  • Agent negligence verified by review
  • Duplicate payment

4. Structured Review Process

All refund requests undergo structured review. Refunds are not processed based on emotional or verbal claims.

Refunds, where applicable, shall apply only to the platform service charge component. Government fees are non-refundable unless refunded by the respective authority.

5. Processing Timeline

Approved refunds shall be processed within 7–10 business days via original payment method.

6. Abuse Protection

Repeated or fraudulent refund claims may result in account suspension.
